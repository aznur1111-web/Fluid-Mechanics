Honest scope

Calculators use standard SI relationships and common engineering approximations — Swamee–Jain and Colebrook for Colebrook–White, ideal-gas isentropic relations, rigid-column and Joukowsky water hammer, empirical Manning and weir coefficients. Fluid properties and loss coefficients are your inputs. The lab is a teaching simulation in model units, not a validated CFD solver. Results are for education, preliminary design and quick checking — not certified design.
